# Session — 2026-09-30 — Camera punch-in on crit snaps

## Goal
During the dice snap-to-face phase, if a die finishes on its max face (crit —
"an 8" on a d8), zoom the camera onto that die for a dramatic effect.

## Change (main direct, commit `727d383`)
- `src/dice3d.js` (inside `startSim`):
  - `critZoom` slot set in `snapDie` when the face being snapped to is the
    die's max face (`rollValueForGroup(...) === d.def.sides`) — first crit
    wins, later crits don't re-aim the camera;
  - in `animate()`, the camera eases from its throw-start position to a
    close top-down shot (3.2 units above the crit die) over
    `SNAP_DURATION_MS` (600 ms, same cubic-out easing as the snap), keeps
    lookAt on the die, and holds the shot until the popup closes;
  - no reset logic needed — each throw/stage re-opens the popup with a fresh
    camera.
- The zoom coexists with the gold crit glow pulse on the die.
