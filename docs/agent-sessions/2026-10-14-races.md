## Decisions

- Traits text lives in `characterSheet.js` (`RACE_TRAITS`) so the reducer bundle
  (deck.js is symlinked into the Supabase function) stays lean; a test keeps the
  two key sets in sync.
- Racial token bonuses are **cap-based** (`maxTokensFor` = base + bonus): the
  stored `maxTokens` never bakes the bonus in, so a race change can't desync the
  pool (`SET_RACE` applies the +2/−2 delta to current tokens and clamps).
- Nain's PV bonus is display-only (`racePvBonus`): `derived.pvMax` is a manual
  field and writing the bonus into it would double-count on every save.
- Advantage rolls are 2d20 keep-1: `rollD20Keep` (flat) or a hand-built
  `{terms:[{count:2,sides:20}], modifier, keep}` intent (3D) since
  `parseDiceSpec` has no keep concept.
- Combat formulas the app cannot verify (e.g. Demi-Elfe catalyst parry) were
  deliberately left as text.
